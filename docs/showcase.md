# Community Showcase & Gallery

We love seeing physical fuse bead creations, pixel-art crafts, and mini-mosaics designed with Bead Grid Studio!

---

## How to Share Your Project

You can share your work directly on GitHub to be featured in our official gallery:

1. Open a new [Community Showcase Issue](https://github.com/zwhy149/bead-grid-studio/issues/new?template=showcase.yml).
2. Include:
   - **Title**: Name of your creation (e.g., *Retro Arcade Ghost*, *Botanical Monstera Leaf*).
   - **Photo**: A high-resolution photo of your finished physical bead piece or screenshot.
   - **Board Dimensions**: e.g., 29x29 mini pegboard, 52x52 large board.
   - **Colors Used**: e.g., MARD-221 (8 colors: H7, H2, A14, etc.) or custom palette.
   - **Pattern File**: Attach or paste the exported pattern JSON file conforming to `schemas/pattern.schema.json`.
   - **Author Attribution**: Your social handle, portfolio, or nickname.

---

## What We Look For

- **Physical Crafts**: Photos of ironed or assembled fuse-bead art.
- **Novel Palettes**: Custom curated color sets (e.g., pastel kits, retro gaming kits).
- **Educational / Workshop Projects**: Photos of classroom, family, or craft market activities using Bead Grid Studio.
- **Ecosystem Tools**: Websites, mobile apps, or scripts utilizing `@bead-grid/core`.

---

## Showcase Guidelines

- **Family-Friendly**: Submissions must adhere to general community standards.
- **Original / Creative Work**: Respect original creators and credit source artwork where applicable.
- **Privacy First**: Do not include personally identifiable information in photos.
